Privacy

What the site processes, which services it uses, and the choices available to visitors.

Last updated August 12, 2026

Information this site processes

NCHurricane.com does not ask visitors to create accounts, submit payment information, or provide personal profiles. Like most web servers, the hosting environment may retain limited technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and response status for reliability and security.

Analytics

With your permission, NCHurricane.com uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to understand page visits, navigation, scrolling, and link interactions so the site can be evaluated and improved. Google Analytics may process information such as the page visited, approximate location, browser and device information, and interaction events. NCHurricane.com does not send names, email addresses, or account information because the site does not collect them.

The Google Analytics tag does not load until you select Allow analytics. If you decline on your first visit, no Analytics request is sent. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled. Analytics event and user data may be retained for up to 14 months.

You can review or change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences in the site footer. Changing your choice to Decline prevents additional Analytics collection on subsequent page views and removes available Analytics cookies for this site. For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy.

External services

Pages request data or media from public weather services and content-delivery networks, including the National Weather Service, National Hurricane Center, NOAA/NESDIS, NWS radar, Leaflet, D3, and Font Awesome. Those providers may receive ordinary connection information when your browser requests their resources.

Outbound links

Links to official agencies, social networks, and ChuckCopeland.com take you to sites with their own privacy practices. NCHurricane.com does not control those services.

Advertising and sale of data

NCHurricane.com does not sell visitor information and does not currently run behavioral advertising.
